Dharamshala Stay: “house of dharma” logos

Dharamshala = dharam (dharma, the spiritual path) + shala (house, shelter): the old name for a rest house for pilgrims. These logos draw that meaning with the Dharma Wheel from the monastery roofs of McLeod Ganj, the Dhauladhar peaks, and a home.

The name, drawn: a house (shala) under the Dhauladhar peaks, with the Dharma Wheel (dharam) at its heart. One look explains "Dharamshala Stay".
